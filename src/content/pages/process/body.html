03 / REFINE & TESTSTEP 3 OF 5

Consider every detail.

Align the formula with its packaging and agree on the testing needed before scale-up.

Review the container, closure, dispensing and decoration together. Define stability, compatibility and other required assessments for the product and intended market.

  • ↗PET, HDPE and glass components, dispensing and decoration
  • ↗Heat, light, humidity, cold and freeze/thaw exposure planning

Bring it to life.

Coordinate the approved formula, components and filling requirements for the production run.
