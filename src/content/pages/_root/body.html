BEFORE WE BEGIN

A little clarity
goes a long way.

All your questions
What is the minimum order quantity?+

Minimum quantities depend on the formula, packaging, filling setup and project scope. Include your target quantity in your brief so the requirements can be reviewed.

Can I start with just an idea?+

Yes. Begin with your intended customer, product type and the experience you want to create. Reference products and ingredient preferences can help make the first discussion more specific.

How long does development take?+

Timing depends on formulation complexity, sample revisions, testing and component availability. A project schedule can be discussed once those dependencies are clear.

Can formula and packaging be developed together?+

That is the preferred planning approach. Consider fill compatibility, dispensing, material choice and component availability during development.
