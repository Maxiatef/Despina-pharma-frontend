Fulfillment & dispatch.
Considered from the start.

Consumer parcels, wholesale cartons and campaign pallets have different needs. Map the order flow, presentation and receiving requirements before the goods are ready.

01

Distribution paths

  • Direct-to-consumer orders
  • Retail, spa, salon and medical-office orders
  • Amazon preparation and shipment plans
  • Subscription-warehouse pallets
  • Launch kits and finished-stock storage
02

Inside the parcel

  • Tissue wrapping
  • Marketing inserts and thank-you cards
  • Crinkle, craft or tissue filler
  • Promotional material and cross-sell samples
03

Outside the parcel

  • Branded mailers or retail-style boxes
  • Custom tape and printed tissue
  • Outer-box stickers and shipping labels
