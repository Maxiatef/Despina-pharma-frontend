04 / PRODUCTION & ASSEMBLY

Contract filling.
Considered from the start.

Start with the product and its container. A filling discussion can begin with prepared bulk or with a formula and raw materials that also need compounding.

01

Pack formats

  • Tubes, jars and bottles
  • Airless pumps and tottles
  • Multi-use pouches and sachet bags
  • Square and oval containers
  • Vials and syringe-style packs
02

Labels and finishing

  • Wraparound, front/back and top/bottom labels
  • Oval and square labels
  • Induction seals and shrink wrapping
  • Multicolor batch and expiry identification
03

Assembly and review

  • Retail cartons and multi-product kits
  • Amazon identifiers and bundles
  • Microbiological test requirements
  • Fill-method and batch-documentation review
PREPARE THE CONVERSATION

Make the next step specific.

Prepare samples of both product and packaging for the quotation discussion. Include target fill volume, quantity, closures, labeling and case-pack details. Confirm which components arrive ready to use, who supplies each item and what must be approved before the run is scheduled.
