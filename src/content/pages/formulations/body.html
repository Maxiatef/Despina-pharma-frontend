FROM CONCEPT TO FORMULA

Make the brief specific.

Product names help you explore. A usable formulation requires product-specific development, documentation and evaluation.

01

Ingredient direction

Record ingredients you would like considered, exclusions, fragrance preferences and the markets you plan to enter.

02

Experience & packaging

Describe application, texture, finish, fill size and dispenser choices. Keep formula and pack decisions connected.

03

Evidence & readiness

Agree on stability, compatibility, microbiological review, labeling and scale-up responsibilities for the project.
