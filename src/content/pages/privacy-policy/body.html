Privacy

How Despina Pharma uses the information you send us.

Who we are. Despina Pharma develops and manufactures beauty and personal-care products. For any privacy question, write to us through the contact page.

What we collect. When you send a form (contact, company profile, product brief, sample request or sample feedback) we receive what you type: your name, e-mail, phone, company, job title, country, your project details and any files you upload. We also record the date, the page you sent it from, your IP address and browser, and your privacy and newsletter choices. If you have a client account we keep your sign-in details (your password is stored only in scrambled form), your projects, messages, quotes, samples, approvals and documents.

Why we use it. To answer your enquiry, prepare quotes and samples, run your project, send you updates and reminders about items waiting for you, keep the service secure (for example blocking spam and repeated wrong sign-ins), and keep records we must keep by law. We send newsletters only if you ticked that box. We do not sell your information and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Who handles it for us. Our website and database host and our e-mail delivery service process your information only to run this service for us. Your information is not shared with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it. Enquiries and project records are kept while we work together and afterwards for as long as needed for business and legal records. Security and activity logs are kept for one year. Newsletter consent lasts until you withdraw it.

Your choices. You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it or delete it, withdraw newsletter consent, or stop reminder e-mails with the link in each reminder. Use the contact page; we answer within one month.
