COMMON QUESTIONS

Clarity before
the next step.

Explore the decisions behind development, quality, production and the finished-product handoff.

What quantity should I plan for?+

Start with the quantity you expect to sell and the pack size you have in mind. The practical production minimum depends on the formula, filling setup and component order. Include a target and an acceptable range in your brief.

How should I plan the development timeline?+

Work backward from the intended launch, allowing separate time for sample reviews, testing, components and production. A requested date is a planning input; a confirmed schedule follows review of the full scope and its dependencies.

What is the first step?+

Prepare a company profile and a separate product brief for each item. Capture the decisions you have made and identify what is still open. These records make the next conversation more focused.

Can I begin without manufacturing experience?+

You can begin with a clear description of the customer and the product experience you want to create. Use the forms to organize that idea, then work through formula, packaging, testing and production questions one stage at a time.

How do I define clean or cruelty-free requirements?+

List your ingredient exclusions, chosen retailer criteria and documentation requirements explicitly. Broad labels can mean different things to different audiences. Agree on the exact criteria and the evidence needed before developing product claims.

How can I arrange a facility discussion or visit?+

Include the purpose of the visit, the areas you would like to understand and your preferred timing in your inquiry. Access, location and availability need to be confirmed with your project contact before making travel plans.

What belongs in a development budget?+

Allow for formulation work, revisions, materials, packaging evaluation and the agreed testing scope. Record whether variations in scent or color are separate items. Development and production costs should be clear before a project begins.

What information supports a useful quote?+

Share the formula direction, fill size, quantity, packaging, assembly needs, testing and delivery destination. A quote should state its assumptions, included services and exclusions so you can compare the complete project cost.

When are payment terms agreed?+

Payment milestones, deposits, cancellation provisions and any credit arrangements belong in the project agreement. Ask which commitments trigger purchasing or scheduling, and keep the agreed terms with your approved scope.

What should I ask about Good Manufacturing Practices?+

Ask how materials are received, equipment is maintained, batches are recorded and finished goods are released. If a GMP credential is required, request the current document and confirm the facility and activities it covers.

How should facility registration be checked?+

Request the registration information relevant to the actual manufacturing location and your intended market. Keep facility records separate from product-level claims. Your project team should confirm what documentation applies before you rely on it.

How should I review ISO credentials?+

Identify the standard your project requires, then check the certificate holder, site, scope and validity. Different standards address different systems. Use applicable documents as part of the supplier review, alongside the practical quality plan.

What does a Certificate of Analysis cover?+

A batch report should identify the product and batch and relate results to agreed acceptance criteria. Confirm which measurements, methods and release records you need, who prepares them and how the documents accompany your goods.

Which product categories can I explore?+

Explore skincare, haircare, body care, fragrance, grooming, baby care, color cosmetics, oral care, sun and tanning care, specialty care, pet care, ingredients, home products and development programs. Each direction requires an individual scope discussion.

What if I already have a formula?+

Explain who owns or authorizes the formula, what documentation is available and whether a reference sample exists. Manufacturing instructions, ingredient sources and specifications help define the transfer work and any evaluation needed.

How do custom and established-base products differ?+

Custom development begins with a product-specific brief. An established base starts from an existing formula with an agreed range of changes. Compare availability, flexibility, ownership, testing and commercial terms before choosing your starting point.

How do I brief a reformulation?+

Describe the current product and the exact reason for change: performance, feel, ingredient sourcing, packaging or another constraint. Identify what must stay consistent and what may change, then agree on how the revised sample will be judged.

What is the difference between stability and compatibility?+

Stability evaluation considers how a product changes over time and under specified conditions. Compatibility considers the formula together with its container and closure. Both need an agreed protocol, observations and acceptance criteria.

Which testing requirements apply to my product?+

The appropriate plan depends on ingredients, intended use, packaging, market and sales channel. Ask qualified specialists to define the evidence needed for safety, shelf life and claims, and assign responsibility for each assessment.

How long should I allow for testing?+

Ask for a schedule tied to the proposed protocol, including interim observations and final reports. Some work can run alongside other development tasks; decisions that depend on test results should remain open until those results are reviewed.

How is the testing budget set?+

A testing quote should identify the product variants, packaging combinations, conditions, methods and reports included. Confirm what happens if the formula or packaging changes or if further investigation is needed.

Which fill sizes should I include in my brief?+

State the target net contents and units, container capacity and any sample, travel, retail or bulk versions. Each format may need its own filling and packaging review. A container’s nominal capacity is not the same as its intended fill.

When does hot or cold filling matter?+

The formula and selected components determine the process to evaluate. Temperature can affect product flow, pack suitability and the finished appearance. Agree on processing conditions and any scale-up checks before choosing the final components.

How should inventory storage be planned?+

Identify what will be held, approximate pallet volume, required conditions and expected duration. Confirm the included holding period, any later fees, stock rotation and release process in the commercial scope.

What should a fulfillment brief contain?+

Describe order volume, item combinations, destinations, inserts and the presentation expected on arrival. Document the order-data handoff, stock updates, packing checks and who handles exceptions. Confirm which services are included for your project.

How do I prepare a marketplace shipment?+

Identify the destination marketplace, shipment plan and current account-specific instructions. Capture unit identification, protective packaging, carton quantities and shipment labels, then assign responsibility for checking the final pack-out against those requirements.

Can I choose the shipping carrier?+

Record your preferred carrier, service level, account arrangement and destination requirements. Confirm who books the shipment, prepares documents and manages the handoff. The available arrangement depends on the agreed dispatch scope.

When should I expect a response?+

Availability and response timing should be confirmed through your project contact. For a time-sensitive question, include the decision required and its deadline so the request can be assessed in context.

How can I prepare an inquiry?+

Use the contact page to capture your question or choose a company, product or sample form. These tools create a downloadable record on your device. Share the finished document through your established Despina Pharma contact.
