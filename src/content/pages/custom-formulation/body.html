01 / THE FORMULA

Custom formulation & R&D.
Considered from the start.

A useful formula brief brings together the customer, the application and the experience you want to create. Make the important choices visible before development begins.

01

Choose your starting point

  • A new product concept
  • A reference product and a defined direction
  • An existing formula for production review
02

Shape the experience

  • Color, fragrance and texture
  • Viscosity, dispensing and after-feel
  • Ingredient preferences and exclusions
  • Performance goals and intended claims
03

Review and refine

  • Development samples and consolidated feedback
  • Recorded adjustments and formula approval
  • Packaging, stability and production planning
PREPARE THE CONVERSATION

Make the next step specific.

Bring a short description of your customer and the role this product plays in their routine. Rank the qualities that matter most. When reviewing samples, describe what you notice during application and after use; this makes the next decision easier to explain.
