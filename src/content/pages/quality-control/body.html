03 / THE DETAILS

Quality control & testing.
Considered from the start.

Quality planning follows the product from incoming materials to the finished pack. Define specifications, test methods, responsibilities and acceptance criteria for the formula and its intended market.

01

Materials and bulk

  • Material purity and condition
  • pH, viscosity and specific gravity
  • Appearance, color and odor
  • Equipment cleaning and sanitation
02

In-process review

  • Compounding and filling checks
  • Retained batch samples
  • Cap, seal and label inspection
  • Secondary packaging and case packs
03

Testing and records

  • Microbiological testing, including proposed USP 60/61 methods
  • Batch records and traceability requirements
  • Certificate of Analysis scope
  • Specialist laboratory work where required
PREPARE THE CONVERSATION

Make the next step specific.

Describe any unusual ingredients, sensitive components or intended claims at the first discussion. Ask which evaluations happen before scale-up and which support routine release. Keep the approved specification with the current formula and artwork so that each review uses the same reference.
